Modern Treehouse

Design details

This Treehouse Modern just fits your contemporary design infused with nature tastes. Finished elegantly with high end dressed timber or marine ply, it gives a very smooth and polished look that adds to its modernity. It is usually built through a medium thick strong tree or safely-on posts and most often designed to span between two or more larger trees. Adorned with skirting around doors and windows for a classy finish, it could also include a balcony to lounge out on relaxing while surrounded by the beauty of nature. This truly exquisite tree house is a fine mix of elegance, durability, and functionality, making it one of the best suited for large wood areas.

Lists

  • Made from dressed timber or marine ply
  • Have skirting around doors and windows
  • Can include balcony
  • Built in medium-sized sturdy tree or on posts
  • Often built between multiple large sturdy trees
  • Best suited for large, wooded areas
