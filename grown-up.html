Grown-up Treehouse

Design details

The Grown-Up Treehouse is the ideal getaway, boasting the charm of an "in-the-trees" cabin with the comfort of a modern living space. Designed for year-round residents, it is fully insulated and built to current building codes, creating a cozy and weathertight environment during all seasons. This tree house has a huge footprint of up to 50m² and gives access to sufficient relaxation, working or even long stays space, though consent is required for construction. Be it a weekend getaway or a full-time residence, this tree-roofed sanctuary mixes adventure with everyday convenience, the escape into nature you have always wanted.
