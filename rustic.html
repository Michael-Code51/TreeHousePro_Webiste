Rustic Treehouse

Design details

Rustic-inspired yet cozy, the Treehouse is a charming little retreat made from simple rough-sawn timber to integrate naturally with the environment. Made under 5m², this canopy is erected either within a sturdy medium-sized tree or securely fastened onto just a few posts, allowing it to be stable as well as high in experience. Each treehouse comes with a strong roof as protection against the weather, with a set of stairs or a simple ladder to reach easy access, making it a very delightful retreat for relaxation or for adventure.

Lists

  • Made from basic rough-sawn timber
  • All come with roof and stairs or ladder
  • Under 5m²
  • Built in medium-sized tree or on posts
