Fantasy Treehouse

Design details

Our fantasy treehouse is an expansive escape, and an enchanting treasure designed for those desiring a magical retreat among trees. Constructed of durable and weather-proof materials, it finds its foothold on a large, stout tree, thus making it a cacophony of sounds in a sylvan environment. Usually more than 10 square meters, this treehouse will likely need some permission but breathes in lots of space for comfort and creativity. With optional glazing for light and lockable doors for security, this cozy little haven, which is very safe designates a perfect place for winding down, leisure, or a unique nature-inspired working spot.
